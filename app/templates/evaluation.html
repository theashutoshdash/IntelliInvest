CLASSIFICATION PIPELINE

Extra Trees classifier

Inputs

Age, income, investment horizon, self-reported loss tolerance and investment experience. Values outside observed training ranges are flagged as extrapolation.

Estimator

400 randomized decision trees with balanced class weighting and a fixed random seed. The output is a probability distribution across Conservative, Moderate and Aggressive classes.

Validation

Five-fold stratified cross-validation, a stratified 20% holdout, balanced accuracy, macro F1 and an age-only baseline. These are dataset validation metrics, not verified suitability accuracy for a live population.

Dataset limitation

The included labelled investor data does not document its sampling or labelling protocol. Model scores can reflect patterns in those labels and must not be interpreted as a guarantee.

PORTFOLIO CONSTRUCTION

Historical CVaR optimiser

Long-only constrained optimisation minimises empirical Conditional Value at Risk for aligned daily adjusted returns. Risk-category settings determine confidence level and position constraints. The output is based on the selected historical sample; it is not a forward-return maximiser.

RETURN RESEARCH

Walk-forward model

HistGradientBoostingRegressor uses lagged returns, realised volatility, moving-average distances, RSI and range features. A chronological holdout and TimeSeriesSplit with a purge gap reduce temporal leakage. Results are benchmarked against a zero-return estimate.

MARKET DATA EXTRACTION

Quote and history pipeline

Quote preference

Fast quote fields are requested first. During an open cash session, five-minute bars can be used as a fallback. When no intraday quote is available, the latest adjusted daily close is returned with its own source and observation date.

Resilience

Requests have bounded timeouts and a retry. A last-good in-process cache is preserved on upstream failure rather than being overwritten by an empty response. Dashboard quote snapshots refresh more frequently during market hours.

Refresh limits

The app polls its top market ribbon every 30 seconds during the regular session and every three minutes outside it. Quote caching and upstream throttling mean these are refresh intervals, not a promise of exchange real-time delivery.

Session calendar

The session clock uses Asia/Kolkata time, a 09:00 pre-open window and the 09:15–15:30 regular cash session. Weekends and the published 2026 NSE holidays are handled from data/nse_holidays.json. Update that file from the official NSE holiday calendar when a new annual list is issued; special sessions need their own timetable entry.

Provider

Yahoo Finance is a third-party research-data source. It may delay, revise, omit or throttle data and should not be used as an execution or exchange reference feed.

RESEARCH DISCOVERY

Dynamic stock shortlist

Candidate universe

Each assessment samples candidates from the supported NSE stock universe and the curated large-, mid- and small-cap starter lists. Symbols without sufficient recent daily history are excluded from scoring.

Signals

Ranking uses 20-, 60- and 120-session price momentum, 20-session and 60-session realised volatility, one-year maximum drawdown and available average volume. Signal weights vary by risk category.

Variation

A fresh candidate sample and weighted selection from the strongest available names create a different shortlist on each assessment. Randomness changes candidate coverage, not the observed metrics or signal values.

OPERATING DIAGNOSTICS

Common errors

Market data unavailable

Retry later. The provider may be throttling requests or may not publish intraday data for that instrument. The app retains the last usable cached history when available.

Insufficient price history

Newly listed, illiquid or unsupported symbols may not have enough aligned daily observations for portfolio optimisation or return validation.

OAuth callback failure

Confirm that the Client ID and current Client Secret belong to the active Google Cloud project and that the redirect URI matches exactly, including scheme, host and path.

Database schema mismatch

After changing SQLAlchemy models, create and apply a migration before restarting the production server.

Deployment profile photo loss

Local instance uploads are stored on the application filesystem. A production deployment with an ephemeral filesystem needs object storage or a persistent volume for durable profile photos.
